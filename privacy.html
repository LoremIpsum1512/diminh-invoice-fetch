Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy describes how DI MINH invoice fetch (“the app”), an internal tool of CÔNG TY TNHH TM DI MINH (“the company”), handles Google user data.

What the app accesses

The app requests one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly. It is used only on the company's own accounting mailbox, by people the company authorises. With it the app searches for messages that carry supplier invoices, reads their headers (sender, date, subject), and downloads their attachments.

How the data is used

Downloaded invoice files are used only for the company's bookkeeping, reconciliation with the tax authority's invoice list, and tax records. The app does not send, delete, label or modify any email.

Where the data is stored

Downloaded files and the access token are stored only on the company's own computer. The app has no server of its own and does not copy mailbox data anywhere else.

Sharing

The company does not sell, rent or share Google user data with any third party, and does not use it for advertising.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalised artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Retention and deletion

Invoice files are kept as long as the company's accounting records require. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the company deletes the stored token when access is revoked or no longer needed. To request deletion of downloaded data, contact the address below.
