DI MINH invoice fetch

DI MINH invoice fetch is an internal tool of CÔNG TY TNHH TM DI MINH (tax ID 4201965284), a food distributor in Nha Trang, Vietnam.

It reads the company's own accounting mailbox, with read-only access, to find e-invoices that suppliers send by email and download their attachments (PDF and XML) for the company's bookkeeping and tax records.

  • It is used only by the company, on the company's own computer.
  • It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
  • It never sends, deletes, labels or modifies email.
